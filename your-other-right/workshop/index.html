Your Other Right! Workshop

Design 4D puzzle levels as a matrix of floor plans: one row per Up level, one column per Ana layer. Push blocks onto goals, light targets with mirrors, and use ramps, ladders, holes and ice. The solver checks every edit, and the slice view lets you walk the level as you build it.

View: drag to look · ←→ turn 90° · WASD step · Q/E kata/ana · C/Z climb up/down · R turn mirror · U undo move · 123 pick axis · Space swap · B normal view · V snap view · Editor: Ctrl+Z undo

Level

East North

Each plan is one Up level of one Ana layer; North is up, East is right. Outside the grid counts as wall. A hole on the lowest level is a pit: blocks fill it, players can't survive it. Ramps are two cells long and climb one level; click a ramp again to turn it. Ladders open the floor above them. Ice makes players and blocks slide until something stops them. Click a mirror or emitter again to turn it; violet mirrors tilt into Ana, and their letter says where a beam heading East or North goes. On the plans, ⊙ marks a beam leaving the layer ana and ⊗ kata.

Solver

Waiting for a level.

Moves: e w n s a k (East, West, North, South, Ana, Kata); capitals push a block. u and d climb a ladder. ↻n/ turns the mirror to your north to /. Swapping views is free, so the solver ignores it.

Level text

Each cell is two characters. First, the floor: . floor, ~ ice, o hole, # wall. Second, what's on it: . nothing, B block, G goal, * block on goal, P start, + start on goal, H ladder, x laser, T target, emitters > < ^ v A K, mirrors / \ { } [ ], ramp foot e w n s a k (rising toward that direction) with = on the head. Each layer starts with a line like “up 0 ana -1”; the top row is North. Your work also saves in this browser.
